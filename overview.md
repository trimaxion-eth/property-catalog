## What It Does

You know how small hotels, B&Bs, and vacation rentals usually have two bad options: pay thousands for a custom website, or wrestle with a DIY builder like Wix that takes weeks and still looks amateur?

This replaces both. An accommodation owner answers a few questions about their property, and the system builds them a complete, professional website in minutes — written copy, organized photos, proper layout, the works. They get a real website they can publish on their own web address, not a template they have to figure out.

---

## Who It's For

Independent accommodation owners — boutique hotels, bed & breakfasts, vacation rentals, cabins, villas, small apartment operators. People who don't have a marketing team or a web developer, but need a website that looks like they do.

---

## How It Works (From Their Perspective)

**1\. They answer questions about their property.**  
Simple stuff: property name, type, location, what rooms they have, what amenities they offer, contact details, brand colors. Takes about 10 minutes.

**2\. The system builds the site automatically.**  
It writes the descriptions, organizes the photos into galleries, lays out the pages, sets up the navigation, and structures everything for Google. The owner doesn't write any copy or design anything.

**3\. They review and tweak.**  
They can regenerate any section they don't like, swap photos, adjust details. Everything is editable but nothing _requires_ editing.

**4\. They publish.**  
They connect their own domain (like www.theirhotel.com) and the site goes live. The system handles the security certificate automatically — no technical setup on their end.

## What The Website Includes

**Pages that every accommodation site needs:**

- Homepage with property highlights and a clear call to action
- Individual room pages with descriptions, photos, and pricing
- Photo gallery organized by category
- Location page with map and nearby attractions
- Contact page with form and details

**Built-in essentials:**

- Works perfectly on phones (where most guests browse)
- Loads fast
- Shows up properly in Google searches
- Professional, trustworthy design

**Booking options:**

- Starts simple — a "Book Now" button that sends them to wherever the owner already takes bookings (email, WhatsApp, their Booking.com page)
- Later, a real booking system with availability calendar and online payment gets added

The owner doesn't _build_ a website. They _describe their property_, and the system builds it for them. That's the entire pitch.

Most website builders give you a blank canvas and tools. This one asks questions and hands you a finished product.
